ATTACK CLICK — A+LMB in SMART mode attacks whatever sits nearest your cursor, so you never pixel-hunt a creep's centre. It also keeps going: when that target dies you roll onto the next one in range, so you can hold the wave without re-clicking. S stops it. F2 switches to LEGACY, where it is a plain attack-move. Right-click is always just move — or a single attack, if you click directly on a unit.

GOAL — reach the score first: 2 points in 1v1, 4 in 2v2. A hero kill is 1 point — and dropping the enemy tower wins outright. Towers take only 15% damage while none of your creeps are near them, so bring the wave.

LAST HITTING
The gold ring around your hero fills as your swing recharges — it flashes white when the attack is ready. Attacks land after a short wind-up — moving cancels the swing. Any creep in range shows its HP, and the pale slice on its bar is what your next hit removes. When that hit would kill, the bar turns gold and a chevron appears — it already accounts for your wind-up, your projectile's flight and every blow already on its way, so swing the moment you see gold. A hollow grey chevron means something else kills it first.

TIP — gold only comes from the killing blow. Deny your own creeps below 50% HP to halve enemy XP.
